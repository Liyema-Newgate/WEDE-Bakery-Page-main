Our Story

Wendy’s Sweet Treats is a family-owned bakery based in South Africa. We specialize in handmade cakes, donuts, pastries, cupcakes, and more! Every treat is made fresh daily using high-quality ingredients and a whole lot of love.

We aim to bring joy to households, events, and everyday moments through delicious desserts crafted with passion.
