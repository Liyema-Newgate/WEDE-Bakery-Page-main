About Us

Wendy’s Sweet Treats is a family-owned bakery based in Cape Town, specialising in handcrafted cakes, cupcakes, pastries, and artisanal desserts.

What began in a tiny home kitchen has grown into a thriving bakery serving customers daily.

We pride ourselves on fresh ingredients, classic recipes, and unforgettable flavours.

We use recyclable packaging and locally sourced ingredients wherever possible.
