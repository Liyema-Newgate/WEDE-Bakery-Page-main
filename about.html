our story

wendy's sweet treats started as a small kitchen in cape town — with a mission to craft delicious, handmade desserts using quality ingredients and lots of love.

we prefer simple, fresh ingredients, slow baking and time-tested recipes that make every bite memorable.

we cater for birthdays, small events and local markets. contact us for custom orders.

where possible we source local ingredients and use recyclable packaging.
